# Global Ocean Sea Surface Salinity and Density — NRT Daily

Original name: Multi Observation Global Ocean Sea Surface Salinity and Sea Surface Density : Daily, Near Real Time

## What this dataset is

The Multi Observation Global Ocean Sea Surface Salinity and Sea Surface Density product provides daily, global, gap-free Level-4 (L4) analyses of Sea Surface Salinity (SSS) and Sea Surface Density (SSD) at 1/8-degree resolution. It is generated by blending observations from multiple satellite missions (such as SMOS and SMAP) with in-situ measurements (such as those from Argo floats and TSGs) using a sophisticated multivariate optimal interpolation algorithm, creating a long and consistent time series that includes both historical (reprocessed) data and near-real-time updates. By delivering these climate variables, the dataset is useful for monitoring the global water cycle, studying changes in ocean circulation driven by density variations, and serving as an input for climate modelling and ocean forecasting systems.

This dataset provides the Near-Real-Time (NRT) version of the daily, global, Level-4 analysis for Sea Surface Salinity (SSS) and Sea Surface Density (SSD).

## Coverage and time range

- Coverage: Global ocean
- Time range: 2023-01-01 至 2026-09-08

## Resolution and specifications

- Spatial resolution: about 14,000 m (about 14 km (1/8 degree))
- Data type: ImageCollection, accessed through Google Earth Engine

## What you can use it for

- Monitoring the global water cycle with gap-free salinity fields
- Studying density-driven ocean circulation changes
- Input to climate models and ocean forecasting systems

In Earth Engine:

