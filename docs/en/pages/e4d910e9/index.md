# ERA5 Hourly Pressure Levels — ECMWF Climate Reanalysis

Original name: ERA5 Hourly Pressure Levels - ECMWF Climate Reanalysis

## What this dataset is

ERA5 is the fifth generation ECMWF atmospheric reanalysis of the global
climate. Reanalysis combines model data with observations from across the
world into a globally complete and consistent dataset. ERA5 replaces its
predecessor, the ERA-Interim reanalysis.

This dataset contains 3D parameters at 37 pressure levels.

## Coverage and time range

- Coverage: Global
- Time range: 1940-01-01 至 2026-10-27
- Cadence: Hourly

## Resolution and specifications

- Spatial resolution: about 27,830 m (roughly 31 km grid)

## What you can use it for

- 3D structure analysis of upper-air circulation and weather systems
- Temperature, humidity, wind and divergence studies on pressure levels
- Initial fields and validation for numerical weather prediction

## How to get it

In Earth Engine:

```js
var dataset = ee.ImageCollection('ECMWF/ERA5/HOURLY_PRESSURE_LEVELS');
```

- Earth Engine ID: `ECMWF/ERA5/HOURLY_PRESSURE_LEVELS`
- Providers: Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S), Google Earth Engine
- Official catalog page: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/ECMWF_ERA5_HOURLY_PRESSURE_LEVELS
