# Copernicus Global Ocean Biogeochemical Forecast — CAR

Original name: Copernicus Global Ocean Bio-Geo-Chemical Forecast - CAR

## What this dataset is

This is the CAR package of the Copernicus global ocean biogeochemical analysis and forecast system, run by Mercator Ocean at 1/4-degree resolution. It provides 10 days of 3D global ocean forecasts, updated weekly; the time series is aggregated to form a sliding window of two full years. The system produces daily and monthly mean files of biogeochemical parameters (chlorophyll, nitrate, phosphate, silicate, dissolved oxygen, dissolved iron, primary production, phytoplankton, zooplankton, pH, and surface partial pressure of carbon dioxide) over the global ocean, on 50 vertical levels from 0.49 m down to 5727.92 m depth.

This dataset consists of ocean pH (ph) and dissolved inorganic carbon (dissic), each provided on the 50 depth levels listed in the band table.

## Coverage and time range

- Coverage: Global ocean
- Time range: 2022-01-01 至 2025-09-19

## Resolution and specifications

- Spatial resolution: about 27,750 m (1/4-degree grid; 50 vertical levels from 0.49 m to 5727.92 m)
- Data type: ImageCollection, accessed through Google Earth Engine

## What you can use it for

- Ocean acidification monitoring and research with 3D pH fields
- Carbon-cycle studies using dissolved inorganic carbon
- Short-term (10-day) biogeochemical forecast applications
