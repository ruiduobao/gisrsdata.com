# Copernicus Global Ocean Biogeochemical Forecast — OPTICS

Original name: Copernicus Global Ocean Bio-Geo-Chemical Forecast - OPTICS

## What this dataset is

The operational Mercator Ocean biogeochemical global ocean analysis and forecast system at 1/4 degree provides 10 days of 3D global ocean forecasts, updated weekly. The time series is aggregated in time to reach a two full years' sliding window. The product includes daily and monthly mean files of biogeochemical parameters (chlorophyll, nitrate, phosphate, silicate, dissolved oxygen, dissolved iron, primary production, phytoplankton, zooplankton, pH, and surface partial pressure of carbon dioxide) over the global ocean, displayed at 1/4-degree horizontal resolution in a regular longitude/latitude equirectangular projection, on 50 vertical levels with depths ranging from 0.49 m to 5727.92 m.

This dataset consists of the diffuse attenuation coefficient of light in sea water.

## Coverage and time range

- Coverage: Global ocean
- Time range: 2022-01-01 至 2025-09-26

## Resolution and specifications

- Spatial resolution: about 27,750 m (about 27.75 km (1/4 degree))

- Short-term (10-day) forecast applications
