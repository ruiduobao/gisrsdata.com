## Featured datasets

- [Landsat 8 Level 2 Surface Reflectance and Surface Temperature](/en/pages/778e3960/) — analysis-ready 30 m data, 2013 to present
- [Harmonized Sentinel-2 MSI Surface Reflectance](/en/pages/0620c27d/) — 10 m, about 5-day revisit, harmonized across time
- [Sentinel-1 SAR GRD](/en/pages/5986ee6b/) — C-band radar imagery, works through clouds and rain
- [Copernicus DEM GLO-30](/en/pages/3bdccac6/) — one of the most used global DEMs, at 30 m
- [ASTER Global DEM download guide](/en/pages/7306f8/) — step-by-step download from NASA Earthdata
- [GPWv4.11 Population Count](/en/pages/9cd921b2/) — global gridded population, a base layer for spatial analysis
- [Global Ocean Sea Surface Salinity and Density (daily)](/en/pages/36f5ea1f/) — daily maps, 1993–2024
- [ESA WorldCover 10m v200](/pages/08a038e5/) — ESA global land cover at 10 m *(guide in Chinese)*
- [Dynamic World V1](/pages/a78539ca/) — near-real-time 10 m land cover probabilities from Sentinel-2 *(guide in Chinese)*
- [MOD13Q1.061 Vegetation Indices](/pages/c13f53a4/) — 16-day NDVI/EVI at 250 m *(guide in Chinese)*
- [Hansen Global Forest Change v1.9](/pages/142e537b/) — global forest gain and loss, 2000–2021 *(guide in Chinese)*
- [CHIRPS Precipitation Daily](/pages/7d12c001/) — global daily precipitation, 1981 to present *(guide in Chinese)*
